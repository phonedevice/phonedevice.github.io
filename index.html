How It Works

Mobile Device Test doesn't inspect your phone remotely — it reads the same information your browser already exposes to every website you visit, and displays it back to you in one readable place.

1

Open this page

Standard browser APIs — like navigator, screen and WebGL — start reporting your device's basic specs immediately.

2

Run a check

Tap a tool card to run its test. Some, like touch or GPU, run instantly. Others, like sensors, ask your permission first.

3

Read the results

Each card shows a pass, attention, or unsupported status plus the raw values it detected — nothing is sent off your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mobile Device Test free to use?
Yes. Every diagnostic tool on this site is free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no account or app install.
Does this app collect or upload my device data?
No. All detection runs locally using standard browser APIs. Nothing about your device or its readings is sent to a server or stored outside your session.
Which phones and browsers are supported?
The site works on any modern browser, including iOS Safari, Android Chrome, and desktop Chrome, Edge, and Firefox. Some checks, such as the Battery Status API, are only available in Chromium-based browsers.
Why do some tools ask me to tap a button instead of running automatically?
Checks like motion sensors, battery status and location require a user gesture or a permission prompt from the browser, so those tools wait for you to tap Run Check first.
